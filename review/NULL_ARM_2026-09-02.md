## Analysis to freeze once the original artifact is recovered

Preserve the historical result and reproduce it first. Then freeze a new protocol
with an explicit coefficient ensemble, calibration-only threshold choice,
independent evaluation seeds, paired endpoints and a fixed comparison rule.
Report the full randomized distribution, the canonical model's location in it,
detection rates, lead times including misses, matched episode false alarms, and
uncertainty. Keep all coefficient draws, not only successful ones. Any tuning of
coefficient ranges or choice of endpoint after seeing results is exploratory.

Random coefficients retain the architecture and may retain useful filtering.
Thus above-chance performance alone is not a refutation of the architecture;
it tests the claimed specificity of the chosen coefficients. A meaningful
"chance" reference also requires an explicit outcome model. Strong conventional
controls and calibration without evaluation leakage remain necessary.
